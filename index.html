Questions we hear

Straight answers before the site visit.

Request a site visit
Do you remodel kitchens?

No. We install them. Our kitchen work is labor only: you choose and buy the custom or high-end cabinetry, and we install it, set to a laser line and scribed to your walls. Demolition, plumbing and electrical stay with your remodeler or general contractor.

Can you replace one interior door or one window's trim?

That's not the work we're set up for. Our trim and millwork covers the whole home: interior doors, window casing, baseboard and crown, planned so every room matches. For a single door, a local installer will likely be a better fit, and a faster one.

Is winter a good time for stairs, trim or doors?

Yes. Stairs, trim and interior doors don't take over your daily routine the way a kitchen install does, so the colder months are a good time to get them done.

Why hire a registered, insured company instead of the lowest bid?

Because the risk is in your home. We're licensed and insured, registered in Massachusetts as HIC #219068, and OSHA-trained. Your installation carries a 10-year warranty. An unregistered, uninsured installer can price lower because none of that is in the number.

What does the 10-year warranty cover?

The installation. If something fails because of how it was installed, a defective material, or a poor material choice, we come back and correct it. It doesn't cover damage from misuse. To use it, call or text (978) 596-5623.

Do you supply the materials?

For stairs, railings and built-ins, yes: material and labor. For kitchens, no: you supply the cabinetry and we install it. At the site visit, Ricardo shows you a materials catalog on his iPad.

Do you work in my town?

If it's one of our 60 towns in Massachusetts and Southern New Hampshire, yes, from Wellesley and Weston to Hollis, NH. The full list is above. If yours isn't there, send the request anyway and we'll answer honestly.

How fast do I get a price, and will it change?

Ricardo visits the same day you reach us or the next, and your estimate arrives by email the same day as the visit. The price is fixed. Lindsay calls to follow up and answer questions, not to haggle.
